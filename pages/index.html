Lyre

A calm music player for your own library on GNOME.

Install
  • Install with GNOME Software Opens your app store; updates arrive like any other app

Lyre plays the music you already own, from your own folders. No streaming service, no account, no recommendations — just your albums, tracks, favourites and playlists.

It gets the details right: gapless playback, cover art from your files' own tags, and tag edits written back into the files, so your fixes follow the music to any other player.

How Lyre compares

Lyre is small on purpose. If one of these fits you better, it's a great choice:

AppMade forCompared with Lyre
GNOME Music Playing your Music folder with no setup GNOME's own player, and the simplest start. Lyre adds tag editing and a sleep timer.
Rhythmbox Libraries, internet radio and podcasts, with plugins Does more, with more settings. Lyre is only your own files, in a newer GNOME design.
Amberol Playing a few songs or a folder, with no library Deliberately has no library. Lyre keeps one, with albums, favourites and playlists.
Strawberry Large collections, with many power-user features A Qt app with far more options. Lyre is simpler and fits in with GNOME.

Questions

Which files can Lyre play?

MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, M4A/AAC, WAV and WMA, from your Music folder or any other folder you add.

Does Lyre change my files?

Only when you edit tags: a changed title, artist or album is written back into the file itself, so it shows up the same in any other player. Playing, rating and making playlists never touches your files.

Where does the cover art come from?

From the art embedded in your files first. If an album has none, Lyre looks it up on MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive. Any image you pick yourself always wins.

Does Lyre go online?

Only to fill in missing cover art and artist details, from MusicBrainz, the Cover Art Archive, Wikidata and Wikipedia. No account, and your music is never uploaded.

Can I control it from the keyboard or lock screen?

Yes. Media keys, GNOME's sound menu and the lock screen all work, and the whole app can be driven from the keyboard.

Why does GNOME Software list broad permissions?

Lyre reads and writes your Music folder, so it can play your files and save tag edits into them. It can also reach network shares and phones mounted in Files, and it reads your system's icon theme so its buttons match your desktop. It uses the network only for missing cover art and artist details.

Why isn't Lyre on Flathub?

Flathub doesn't accept new apps made with AI assistance, and Lyre was built with help from Claude. It's published from its own signed repository instead, and updates arrive through GNOME Software or flatpak update like any other app.

How do I install or remove it from a terminal?

To install:

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists lyre \
    https://drvonmiau.github.io/lyre/index.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user lyre io.github.drvonmiau.Lyre

To remove Lyre and its repository:

flatpak uninstall --user io.github.drvonmiau.Lyre
flatpak remote-delete --user lyre
